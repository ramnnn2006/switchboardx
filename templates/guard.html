Each tool is fine.

Chained, they leak your inbox.

An agent that reads private data, takes in text an attacker wrote, and can send things out has all it needs to leak. Any two of those are fine. Switchboard checks the whole plan and cuts the step into the way out before anything runs.

What this won't catch

  • Flags are set at ingest by heuristics. A wrong flag gives a wrong verdict.
  • It checks the plan. It doesn't sandbox the run.
  • A tool's output can still mislead the model inside an allowed chain.

The pattern has a name: Simon Willison's lethal trifecta.
